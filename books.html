Stewardship

Preserving Volumes for Future Generations

Every book in our collection is shared among thousands of scholars. Follow these four care standards.

dry_cleaning

Clean Hands & Bookmarks

Never fold corner pages (dog-earing). Utilize complimentary acid-free ribbon bookmarks provided at circulation desks.

menu_book

Spine & Binding Care

Avoid forcing bindings flat face-down. Support heavy hardcover volumes with desk reading wedges available in reading rooms.

water_drop

Beverage Safeguards

Only sealable spill-proof travel thermoses are permitted in general reading rooms. Absolutely no liquids near rare folios.

healing

Report Loose Pages

Do not apply household adhesive tape. Return damaged volumes to the conservation desk for archival pH-neutral mending.
