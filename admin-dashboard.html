alt_route Section 4: Book Inspection, Restoration & Reshelving Routing Guide

Operational guidelines for physical volume sorting and return cart distribution.

1. Physical Inspection

Check spine alignment, barcode readability, and examine for accidental loose folios or water damage.

2. Reservation Hold Check

If volume has a pending hold, attach a printed 48-hour patron slip and route to Front Hold Shelf 1B.

3. Stacks Re-shelving

Place cleared volume onto designated department sorting trolley for student librarians to re-file by call number.
