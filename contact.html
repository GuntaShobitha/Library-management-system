Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding borrower limits, overdue policies, digital access, and visiting rules.

Standard active member cards permit up to 4 concurrent volume loans. Faculty and accredited researchers may borrow up to 10 volumes. All loans carry a 14-day checkout duration with one online renewal permitted.
If all copies of a book are currently checked out by other readers, click "Reserve" on the catalog page. You will be assigned a priority queue number. As soon as a copy is returned, you will receive an automatic notification and the book will be held at circulation for 48 hours.
For standard general collections, we operate on a grace period policy with friendly digital reminder notices. In this demonstration system, a nominal $0.50 per day sample rate is tracked to illustrate fine handling in the administrative console.
Yes. Active members who have verified their card at the circulation desk can use their digital member QR pass to access the Grand Silent Carrels 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
Rare archives dating before 1850 require consultation in the special collections reading room. Patrons can submit a research consultation request via our contact form at least 48 hours in advance of their planned visit.
Absolutely. Patrons may submit purchase recommendations directly through our Book Catalog page or choose "Book Recommendation" in the electronic dispatch form above. Our curatorial committee evaluates suggestions on a bi-weekly cycle.
